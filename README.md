# 🎨 Algebra 1 — A Four-Week Course

### Make algebra feel less like a puzzle and more like a set of tools.

🟦 **4 weeks** &nbsp; 🟣 **29 short lectures** &nbsp; 🟢 **Unlimited practice and tutor help**

Build confidence with expressions, equations, graphs, systems, and quadratics in a free, self-paced course. Learn one idea at a time, try it out, and ask questions whenever you get stuck.

> **No account needed. Jump in where you are, work at your own pace, and come back to practice as often as you like.**

---

## 🌈 Your four-week roadmap

| Week | Theme | What you’ll explore |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🟦 **1** | **Expressions and linear equations** | Make sense of variables, simplify expressions, and solve linear equations step by step. |
| 🟣 **2** | **Inequalities, functions, and linear graphs** | Work with inequalities and functions, then connect linear equations to their graphs. |
| 🟢 **3** | **Systems, exponents, and polynomials** | Solve systems, use exponent rules, and build fluency with polynomials. |
| 🟠 **4** | **Factoring, quadratics, and radicals** | Factor expressions, solve quadratic equations, and work with square roots and radicals. |

---

## ✨ Learn, practice, and ask why

- **29 short lectures** break the four-week course into approachable lessons.
- **Unlimited practice** lets you keep working through problems and feedback.
- **An interactive tutor** is there for questions, hints, and another explanation.
- **Two homeworks** give you a chance to bring ideas together as you learn.
- **A midterm with 8 questions** and **a final with 10 questions** help you review your progress.
- **A built-in math keyboard** makes it easier to enter equations and mathematical symbols.

## 🧭 Getting started

1. Open the course and choose a week—or jump straight to a topic that interests you.
2. Read a short lesson and try a practice problem.
3. Ask the tutor for help or review the feedback, then keep practicing.
4. Return to any topic whenever you want a refresher.

There’s no sign-up step. The course is free to explore and designed for flexible, independent study.

---

## 💬 A note about the tutor

The tutor is a learning aid, not an infallible authority. AI can make mistakes, so check important steps against the lesson and use your own mathematical judgment.
