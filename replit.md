# Algebra 1 — A Four-Week Course

A free, self-paced Algebra 1 web course with 29 short lectures, unlimited practice, and an interactive tutor. Learners can begin without creating an account.

## Course roadmap

1. **Expressions and linear equations**
2. **Inequalities, functions, and linear graphs**
3. **Systems, exponents, and polynomials**
4. **Factoring, quadratics, and radicals**

The course also includes two homeworks, an 8-question midterm, and a 10-question final. Learners can move between weeks and topics at their own pace.
